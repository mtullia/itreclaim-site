Solutions for MSPs

Your team installs the new equipment. We take care of the old: pickup, data destruction, resale and the records your clients will ask for.

The Challenge for MSPs

Every refresh you deliver leaves old hardware behind. Clients want it gone quickly and safely, and they expect you to handle it. That can mean storage space, staff time and data risk that sit outside your service agreements.

Handling disposal yourself also means building records that show where each device went. Without them, the risk stays with you and your client long after the equipment has left.

What the Program Provides

  • One Contact

    A single ITReclaim contact for every client project, who knows your accounts and how you like to work.

  • Records by Client

    Inventory, chain-of-custody and data destruction records kept separately for each client, ready to pass on.

  • Value Sharing

    Where equipment has resale value, how it is shared is discussed and agreed for each program in writing.

MSP Program Questions

Do we need a minimum volume to join?

No fixed minimum is set. Smaller client projects can often be grouped, so tell us your typical volume and we will suggest how to handle it.

Can records go straight to our clients?

Records are organized by client so you can pass them on. How they are delivered is agreed when the program is set up.

Can we refer a client to you directly?

Yes. Introduce us, and we will scope that client's project with you kept informed at each step, as agreed in your program.
