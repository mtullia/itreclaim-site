Electronics Recycling

Not everything can be resold. For equipment at the true end of its life, we arrange recycling that keeps it out of landfills and on the record.

What Happens to Equipment With No Value

Recycling is the last step, not the first. Here is how an item reaches it.

  1. 01 Step 1

    Checked for Resale

    Every item is first checked for reuse or resale, so nothing with value is recycled by mistake.

  2. 02 Step 2

    Data Handled First

    Any device that stores data goes through sanitization or destruction before it is recycled.

  3. 03 Step 3

    Separated by Material

    Equipment is sorted so metals, circuit boards, plastics and batteries go to the right streams.

  4. 04 Step 4

    Recorded

    The disposition report lists what was recycled, so your records show where it ended up.

Accepted Categories

These are the categories we typically handle. Exclusions and items that need special handling are confirmed when we scope each project.

  • Computers

    Laptops, desktops, thin clients and workstations, working or not.

  • Data Center Equipment

    Servers, storage arrays, racks, network switches and routers.

  • Media and Components

    Loose hard drives, solid-state drives, memory and circuit boards.

  • Displays and Peripherals

    Monitors, docking stations, keyboards, printers and similar devices.

  • Mobile Devices

    Phones and tablets, handled with their data removed or destroyed first.

  • Cables and Power

    Cabling, power supplies and adapters, usually collected with the rest.

Responsible Downstream Handling

Recycling is carried out by downstream processors chosen for the type of material involved. We check their credentials against the needs of your project before any equipment is sent to them.

Batteries, older tube displays and other regulated items are kept separate and sent only to processors set up to handle them. If an item cannot be accepted, we tell you before pickup rather than after.

Recycling Questions

Do you recycle equipment that still works?

Only if it has no realistic resale value or your policy requires it. Working equipment is normally sent for resale first, which typically returns value to you.

Is there anything you will not take?

Some items need special handling or cannot be accepted by the downstream processor for your project. We confirm any exclusions in writing when we scope the work.

Will I know what happened to my equipment?

Yes. The disposition report shows which items were resold and which were recycled, so your records are complete.

Clearing Out Equipment With No Resale Value?

Tell us roughly what you have and where it is, and we will plan how it can be collected and recycled.
