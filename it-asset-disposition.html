IT Asset Disposition

One project for everything you are retiring, from the first inventory to the last record. One ITReclaim project lead runs it and answers for it.

Who It Is For

IT asset disposition fits any organization that retires equipment in batches and needs proof of what happened to it.

  • Refresh Cycles

    IT teams replacing laptops, desktops or servers on a schedule, who need the old fleet cleared and accounted for.

  • Moves and Closures

    Offices relocating, consolidating or closing, with a deadline to empty rooms full of equipment.

  • Decommissioning

    Server rooms and data halls taking racks, storage and network gear out of service.

  • Client Retirements

    Managed service providers retiring hardware for many clients at once. See the MSP program.

What We Manage, Stage by Stage

The eight parts of our service, grouped by when they happen in a project.

  1. Stage 1

    Inventory & Assess

    Know exactly what is leaving and what it may be worth.

    • IT asset intake and lifecycle coordination
    • Equipment inventory and audit management
    • Asset valuation and recovery assessment
  2. Stage 2

    Secure Pickup, Custody & Sanitization

    Protect the data and track every device while it moves.

    • Secure data sanitization and destruction coordination
    • Chain-of-custody tracking
  3. Stage 3

    Audit & Reclaim Value

    Audit every item, reclaim the best value from it and close the books.

    • Equipment testing, refurbishment and remarketing where applicable
    • Responsible electronics recycling
    • Final disposition audit and financial reconciliation

ITReclaim runs your project and is your single contact; qualified partners do the physical processing. How we work.

What You Receive

At the close of every project you get a record set you can hand to an auditor.

  • Asset inventory. Each item listed by type, make, model and serial number or asset tag.
  • Chain-of-custody records. Who handled the equipment, when it moved and where it went.
  • Data disposition records. The method used for each data-bearing device and its result, issued only after the work is complete and validated.
  • Disposition report. Which items were resold, reused or recycled.
  • Financial reconciliation. Resale proceeds, costs and the net amount, set out line by line.

IT Asset Disposition Questions

How large does a project need to be?

There is no fixed minimum. Small batches can often be combined with other pickups, so tell us what you have and we will suggest the most practical route.

Will we get value back from our old equipment?

In most projects, yes. Retired business equipment typically returns some value to the customer. We cannot promise how much in advance, since it varies with the equipment's type, age, quantity, condition and timing.

Can you work with our asset management system?

Yes. Send us your own inventory export and we will reconcile our records against it, so the final report matches the asset tags and serial numbers you already track.

Who handles the equipment physically?

Packing, transport and processing are carried out by qualified partners that we select and oversee for your project. We tell you who does the work before it starts.
