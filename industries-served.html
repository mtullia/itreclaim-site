Who we work with

Industries Served

We focus on organizations that retire technology in volume and need to prove where it went. Our team has also worked across many other industries, and each one has its own pressure points.

Our Primary Focus

  • Managed Service Providers

    You retire hardware for many clients at once. You need a partner who keeps each client's records separate and lets your team stay on billable work.

    See the MSP program

  • Enterprise and Mid-Market IT

    Large refreshes of laptops, desktops and servers need a plan that matches your asset register, clears devices on schedule and closes the loop in your records.

    Request an assessment

  • Data Centers and Hosting

    Decommissioned servers, storage and network gear can carry real value. Timing, careful removal and drive handling decide how much of it you keep.

    Request an assessment

  • Financial and Corporate Offices

    Regular refresh cycles meet strict data-security expectations. You need method records for every drive and a clear trail for internal audit.

    Request an assessment

  • Healthcare and Medical Groups

    Devices across clinics and offices may hold sensitive records. Multi-site pickups and documented data handling support your own compliance work.

    Request an assessment

Our experience

Industries Our Team Has Served

These industries come from our team's past work. They describe experience, not a list of current customers, and no organization is named or implied to endorse us. Browse them with the buttons, or scroll.

  • Virtual Reality and Immersive Technology

    Headsets, tracking gear and graphics workstations turn over quickly, and some may still have resale value.

  • Entertainment and Hospitality Venues

    Game-floor systems, point-of-sale terminals and back-office PCs across many locations need one steady plan.

  • National Non-Profits and Youth Organizations

    Local chapters and volunteers use many small fleets, and donor and member records must be wiped with care.

  • Federal Government Agencies

    Strict handling rules call for clear paperwork at every step and a record for each device.

  • Law Enforcement

    Devices can hold sensitive case and personal data, so removal of that data comes before anything else.

  • Public-Sector Statistics and Census

    Large temporary fleets of field devices come back in bulk and must be cleared quickly once the work ends.

  • State and Local Government

    Offices, schools and public safety teams retire equipment under their own disposal and records rules.

  • Energy and Oil and Gas Field Operations

    Rugged laptops and tablets come in from remote sites, often in batches that are hard to schedule.

  • Airlines and Aviation

    Operations, crew and gate devices run on tight schedules, so pickups must fit around flight operations.

  • Automotive and Vehicle Remarketing

    Yard scanners, office PCs and network gear move between sites as locations open, close and consolidate.

  • Global E-Commerce and Logistics

    Warehouse scanners, workstations and network gear are retired in volume across many sites.

  • Financial Services

    Frequent refresh cycles and audit rules mean every drive needs a method record and a clear trail.

  • Technology

    Engineering laptops, lab gear and test hardware are retired quickly and may hold proprietary code.

  • Transportation and Travel

    Fleets, depots and ticketing sites share equipment that must be cleared before it leaves.

  • Retail and Point of Sale

    Registers, kiosks and store networks are replaced in waves, often across many small sites.
