Helpful Details to Include

You do not need all of these to get started, but each one speeds up our reply.

  • The types of equipment and a rough count of each
  • Where the equipment is, and whether there is more than one site
  • Whether any devices hold data that must be destroyed
  • Your timing, such as a move date or the end of a lease
  • Any records your organization needs at the end
