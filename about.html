About ITReclaim

ITReclaim is an independent IT asset disposition business focused on corporate end-of-life technology: secure data disposition, value recovery, resale and responsible recycling.

Who We Are

Organizations replace technology on a cycle. The old equipment often ends up in a storage room, where it holds data and loses value every month. We give IT teams one partner they can hold to account for that last stage of an asset's life.

You deal with one company. We plan the project, line up the people who do the physical work, and hand back records that show what happened to each asset.

How We Work

ITReclaim is your account and project-management contact. Physical processing, data sanitization, logistics and downstream disposition may be carried out by qualified partners, including an established ITAD facility partner.

  • We Run the Project

    We scope the work, set the schedule, and stay your single point of contact from the first call to the final report.

  • Partners Do the Physical Work

    Specialist partners handle transport, processing and recycling. Who does each part is written down for every project.

  • You Get One Audit Trail

    We gather the inventory, custody, data and financial records into one package for your audit.

Where We Work

Wherever your equipment is, coast to coast, we coordinate pickup, sanitization and value recovery. One accountable team plans the project, and one point of contact keeps every location on the same plan and the same records.

Logistics are coordinated through vetted partners and confirmed for each project, so you know who does what, and where, before anything moves.

Our Standards Approach

ITReclaim coordinates secure and responsible IT asset disposition through qualified downstream service providers, with certifications and capabilities verified against the requirements of each project.

A certification belongs to the facility that holds it. If your project needs a certain standard, we check that the provider doing that part of the work holds it for that site and scope. We do this before work begins.

Before pickup, you receive the processing partner's current certificate and scope documentation for the work involved, so you can review it against your own requirements.

Tell Us What You Need to Retire

Whether it is one office or a full refresh, we will start by understanding what you have and what you need.
