IT Asset Recovery & Remarketing

Most retired business equipment still returns some value to its owner. We find the items that can be resold, prepare them for market and return the proceeds to you on a statement you can check.

What Drives Value

Five things shape how much value comes back. Recovery cannot be promised in advance, because it depends on all five.

  • Type

    Business laptops, servers and network gear tend to hold value better than peripherals.

  • Age

    Newer generations sell more readily. Value drops with each year a model is out of production.

  • Quantity

    Matched lots of the same model attract more buyers than mixed single items.

  • Condition

    Working units with chargers and no damage sell for more than parts-only units.

  • Timing

    Equipment stored for months loses value. Moving it soon after retirement helps.

How Recovery Works

  1. Step 1

    Review the List

    We go through your equipment list and identify the items most likely to resell.

  2. Step 2

    Test and Grade

    Each item is checked for function and condition and given a grade that sets its route.

  3. Step 3

    Prepare for Sale

    Data is removed first. Then units are cleaned and, where it pays, refurbished.

  4. Step 4

    Sell and Report

    Items are sold through resale channels, and every sale is recorded against its asset.

How Settlement Works

Pricing is set for each project, not taken from a rate card. Depending on the job, the structure may be a share of resale proceeds, a coordination fee, a per-item charge or a combination, and it is agreed in writing before anything is collected.

When the project closes, your settlement statement lists every item, its outcome and any sale proceeds, along with the costs set against them and the net amount. Resale value is never promised in advance because it depends on the market at the time of sale.

Value Recovery Questions

Will I get value back from my retired equipment?

Most retired business equipment typically returns value to you, and newer business-grade gear often returns the most. We cannot promise a result in advance, because what comes back depends on the type, age, quantity, condition and timing of your equipment.

After we review your list, we give you a realistic view of what to expect, and your settlement statement shows what each item actually returned.

Is data removed before anything is resold?

Yes. No device is resold until its data has been removed and the result recorded. Devices that cannot be wiped are destroyed instead. See our data security approach.

What happens to items that do not sell?

They go to responsible recycling, and the disposition report shows that outcome. Read about recycling.
