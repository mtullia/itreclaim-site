Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website collects, how ITReclaim uses it and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect only what you choose to send through the assessment request form: your organization's name, your name, your email address and phone number, the location of the equipment, details about the equipment and your timeline, and any message you add.

Please do not send passwords, account details or any data stored on the devices you plan to retire.

How We Use It

We use these details to respond to your request, to scope and plan a project if you go ahead, and to keep the business records that a project requires. We do not sell your information.

Who We Share It With

If you go ahead with a project, we share only the details needed with the partners who carry out the work, such as the pickup location and a site contact. The service providers that host this website and receive the form submissions for us can see what you send, and they may use it only to provide those services. Form submissions are processed and forwarded to us by email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Otherwise we share information only where the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

Requests that do not lead to a project are reviewed at least once a year and deleted or marked do not contact when they are no longer needed. Records for completed projects, including custody and financial records, are generally kept for seven years. Records may be kept longer where a law, a contract or a legal hold requires it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to stop receiving messages from us or to have it deleted. We will act on your request unless we are required to keep the information, in which case we will tell you why.

How We Protect It

Access to the information you send is limited to the people who need it to respond to you, and it is stored on secured systems.

Cookies and Tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising trackers, third-party scripts or third-party fonts.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to us at micah.tullia@itreclaim.com.
