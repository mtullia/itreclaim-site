What We Do

ITReclaim delivers comprehensive IT asset disposition solutions, helping organizations securely retire technology, recover equipment value, and responsibly manage end-of-life assets.

Most jobs use more than one of these services. We plan them as one project.

  • 01

    IT Asset Disposition

    Plan and manage the full retirement of laptops, desktops, servers and network gear, from first inventory to final audit.

  • 02

    Data Sanitization & Destruction

    Wipe or physically destroy data-bearing media, matched to the media type and your rules, with a record for each asset.

  • 03

    IT Asset Recovery & Remarketing

    Test, refurbish and resell equipment that still has market value, then settle the proceeds on a clear statement.

  • 04

    Electronics Recycling

    Send equipment with no resale value to responsible downstream recycling instead of a landfill or a storage room.

How a Project Runs

Five steps, in the same order on every job. Each one leaves a record you keep.

No.StepWhat happensOn the record
  1. 01 Step 1

    Inventory & Assess

    We list each item you are retiring, flag every device that holds data and mark what can likely be resold.

    On the record: Asset list with serials

  2. 02 Step 2

    Secure Pickup & Chain of Custody

    Equipment is packed, sealed and counted at your dock, then moved under an audited chain of custody.

    On the record: Signed custody log

  3. 03 Step 3

    Sanitize

    Each drive is wiped or physically destroyed. The method is set by the media type and your data rules.

    On the record: Sanitization record per drive

  4. 04 Step 4

    Audit

    Every serial number is checked against the starting list. Anything missing or failed is listed, not left out.

    On the record: Audit report, exceptions listed

  5. 05 Step 5

    Reclaim Value

    Equipment with market value is resold and the proceeds are reconciled line by line. The rest goes to recycling.

    On the record: Settlement statement

Common Questions

What kinds of equipment do you take?

Laptops, desktops, servers, storage, network gear, monitors, phones, tablets and loose drives are typical. Some items, such as certain batteries, need special handling, so we confirm what is included when we scope your project.

Can you handle equipment spread across several states?

Yes. We coordinate projects coast to coast, and organizations with many sites keep one point of contact and one set of records for all of them. See how we work nationwide.

How is pricing set?

Each project is priced on its own. Quantity, equipment type, resale value, data destruction needs and logistics all play a part, so we review the details and explain the terms in writing before work starts. Most retired equipment returns some value to you, though we cannot promise it in advance.

What audit records do I get at the end?

An asset-level inventory audit, chain-of-custody records, data sanitization or destruction records for the media involved, and a final reconciliation. See the full list.
